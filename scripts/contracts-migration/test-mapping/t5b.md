# T5b test mapping: RewardsManager calculations and distribution

New tests are in `packages/issuance`, on T5a's `RewardsManagerSharedTest` (the RewardsManager Hardhat artifact behind a GraphProxy, wired to `MockGraphToken`, `RMCurationMock` and `RMIssuerMock` as the subgraph service). `curation.mint` becomes `_signal`, `curation.burn` becomes `curation.unsignal`, `staking.allocateFrom` becomes `_allocate`, `closeAllocation` becomes `_close`, and `helpers.mine(n)` / `helpers.mineEpoch` become `vm.roll` (an epoch is 10 blocks, the length the old `takeRewards` setups set). Old `mineEpoch` calls that only aligned to an epoch boundary are dropped, since nothing here reads the epoch. The separate stake/allocate transactions become a one-block roll between signal and allocation, so the pre-allocation block is reclaimed (`NO_ALLOCATED_TOKENS`) as before. Expected values are derived from the explicit rolls with `_expectedRewardsPerSignal`, `_expectedSubgraphRewards` and `_expectedRewardsPerAllocatedToken` (shared.t.sol), and asserted exactly where the old tests used `toRound`. No ported case allocates zero tokens. `test/unit/rewards-manager/calculations.t.sol::test_Rewards_ZeroWhenNoBlocksElapsed` is Review Focus 4 and has no old test.
