## Coverage

`contracts/rewards/RewardsManager.sol` (checklist closed). The globals loop over `packages/issuance/test` prints no `MISSING`. A stricter loop over `test/unit/rewards-manager/*.t.sol` alone (no mocks) also finds every external/public name in `functionsHit` called directly as `rm.<fn>(` or through the proxy init data: `takeRewards`, `reclaimRewards`, `beforeIssuanceAllocationChange` and `onSubgraphSignalUpdate` are also called directly, not only through the mocks. `onSubgraphSignalUpdate` is further reached on every `_signal` (`RMCurationMock.signal` / `unsignal` call it first, as `Curation.mint` / `burn`). `initialize` is called directly in `config.t.sol` and runs in every `setUp` via the proxy init data.

Internal functions and the modifier in the list are reached through public paths:

- `_calcAllocationRewards`: `takeRewards`, `reclaimRewards`.
- `_calcRewards`: `getRewards`, `takeRewards`.
- `_deniedRewards`: `takeRewards` on a denied subgraph or ineligible indexer (`distribution.t.sol`, `eligibility.t.sol`, `reclaim.t.sol`).
- `_getNewRewardsPerSignal`: `getNewRewardsPerSignal`, `updateAccRewardsPerSignal`.
- `_getSubgraphAllocatedTokens`, `_getSubgraphRewardsState`: `getAccRewardsForSubgraph`, `getAccRewardsPerAllocatedToken`, both hooks.
- `_reclaimRewards`: the reclaim tests (`reclaim.t.sol`, `legacySuite.t.sol`).
- `_setDenied`: `setDenied`.
- `_setIssuancePerBlock`: `setIssuancePerBlock`.
- `_updateSubgraphRewards`: both hooks (`snapshotInversion.t.sol` also from an inverted snapshot).
- `onlySubgraphAvailabilityOracle`: `setDenied`, including `test/unit/rewards-manager/config.t.sol::test_SubgraphAvailabilityService_RevertWhen_DenyNotOracle`.

`contracts/staking/libs/MathUtils.sol`:

- `diffOrZero`: reached through `getAccRewardsPerAllocatedToken` (and so `getRewards`, `takeRewards`).
- `min`: reached only through legacy Staking.
- `weightedAverageRoundingUp`: reached only through legacy Staking.
