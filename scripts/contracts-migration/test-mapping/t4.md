## Coverage

MISSING initialize: `EpochManager.initialize` is called through the proxy init data (`acceptProxyAndCall`) built with `abi.encodeCall` in `EpochManagerTest.setUp`, not by a `.initialize(` call; every test runs it, and `test_Configuration_SetEpochLength` asserts its result (`epochLength() == 60`).

Additions with no old test: `test_Configuration_RevertWhen_EpochLengthSame` (`Epoch length must be different to current`), `test_Calculations_RevertWhen_BlockHashNotPast` and `test_Calculations_RevertWhen_BlockHashTooOld` (`blockHash` reverts), `test_Calculations_EpochsSince` (`epochsSince`, `epochsSinceUpdate`, `currentEpochBlockSinceStart` after rolls), and `test_EpochManager_SameBlockDoesNotAdvance` (same block, same epoch and epoch block). `runEpoch` and `isCurrentEpochRun` are covered by the ported progression tests.
