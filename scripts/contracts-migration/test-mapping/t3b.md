# T3b test mapping: L2GNS

New tests are in `packages/subgraph-discovery`. Each `describe` of `l2GNS.test.ts` has its own file under `test/unit/l2-gns/` (`onTokenTransfer with invalid codes` lives in `ReceiveSubgraphFromL1.t.sol`). The `lib/gnsUtils.ts` helpers, with their assertions, and the L1 transfer helpers are in `test/unit/shared/GNSHelpers.t.sol`, on top of `DiscoveryTest`. L1 deliveries go through `L2GatewayMock.deliver`, which stands in for `L2GraphTokenGateway.finalizeInboundTransfer`. Random metadata and IDs are fixed values. `test/unit/Setup.t.sol::test_Setup_PublishNewSubgraph` checks the publish helper and has no old test.

## Coverage

Checked against `coverage-baseline.json` for `contracts/l2/discovery/L2GNS.sol`, `contracts/discovery/GNS.sol`, `contracts/discovery/SubgraphNFT.sol`, `contracts/base/Multicall.sol` and `contracts/utils/TokenUtils.sol`.

MISSING initialize: `GNS.initialize` is called by `DiscoveryTest` through `abi.encodeCall` in the proxy init data (`acceptProxyAndCall`), so every test runs it.

MISSING setSubgraphMetadata: `SubgraphNFT.setSubgraphMetadata` is `onlyMinter` (the minter is GNS) and is reached through `gns.publishNewSubgraph` and `gns.finishSubgraphTransferFromL1`. Likewise `SubgraphNFT.mint` and `burn` (the grep matches `.mint(`/`.burn(` on GRT and curation) are reached through `publishNewSubgraph` / the L1 subgraph callhook and `deprecateSubgraph`. `SubgraphNFT`'s constructor, `setMinter` and `setTokenDescriptor` run in `DiscoveryTest.setUp`.

MISSING burnTokens, pullTokens, pushTokens: `TokenUtils` is an internal library. `pullTokens` is reached through `gns.mintSignal`, the owner tax in `gns.publishNewVersion` and `curation.mint`; `burnTokens` through `curation.mint` with a non-zero curation tax (the `PublishNewVersion` setup and pre-curation); `pushTokens` through `curation.burn` (`publishNewVersion`, `burnSignal`, `deprecateSubgraph`, and T3a's L2Curation burn tests).

GNS functions not in the baseline (`setSubgraphNFT`, `setDefaultName`, `updateSubgraphMetadata`, `transferSignal`, `withdraw`, `migrateLegacySubgraph`, `subgraphSignal`, `subgraphTokens`, `isPublished`, `isLegacySubgraph`, `getLegacySubgraphKey`) were only exercised by the L1GNS suite in `gns.test.ts`, which was `describe.skip` and is dropped.

Additions with no old test: `test/unit/l2-gns/Multicall.t.sol::test_GNS_Multicall` (`multicall` is in the baseline; publishes a subgraph and mints signal in one batch, as the skipped L1GNS `batch calls` test did).
